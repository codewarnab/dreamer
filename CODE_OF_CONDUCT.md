# Code of conduct

Dreamer contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces respectful, useful, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and direct.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while discussing disagreements in good faith.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repetition, and the safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/codewarnab/dreamer/security/advisories/new), with "Code of conduct" in the title. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Reports will be handled as privately as practical; information may be shared only with people needed to investigate and respond.
